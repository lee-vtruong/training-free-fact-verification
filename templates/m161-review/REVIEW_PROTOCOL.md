# Independent source-correction review

M1.6.1 creates no human decisions. Export actual sheets with `export-reviews`; placeholder fields remain null. Do not sign a software fixture, an assistant-generated decision, another person's sheet, or a candidate you have not checked.

The two JSON field templates in this directory illustrate editable fields only. They are not complete, importable reviews/candidates. Export concrete source-ID/context/hash-bound sheets from the selected bank; both 50-row independent sheets have been prepared in the local outputs directory without filling any human identity or decision.

1. Pick one immutable bank revision. Check `record_sha256`, source ID, Base raw generation, source gold and all eight displayed evidence passages. Source-only official gold is permitted; gold QA/justifications are absent and must not be pasted in.
2. Candidate authoring is a separate step: fill only explanation, citation spans and proposed mechanisms in an annotation file. Offsets are zero-based Unicode character offsets into `rendered_text`, end-exclusive. A citation quote must equal that exact slice. Use enough spans to support the explanation; do not cite text truncated out of the displayed evidence. Keep explanations null if evidence cannot justify them.
3. Apply candidates to a NEW bank file, then export NEW reviewer_1/reviewer_2 sheets from it. Earlier sheets become stale. Two reviewers receive the same candidate revision independently, without seeing each other's decisions.
4. Each sheet has one real reviewer identity. Fill `reviewer_type="human"`, `independent_review=true`, timezone-qualified `reviewed_at`, decision/reason and the grounding questions. `confirmed_mechanisms` contains only candidate mechanisms actually supported by the evidence. Describe the failure category explicitly; a Base mismatch alone is not proof of a reasoning mechanism.
5. Decisions: VERIFIED only when the candidate explanation, source gold relation and evidence sufficiency are all supported; INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE when the displayed retrieval cannot support the gold correction; REJECTED for an incorrect/unsupported explanation. Engineering parser errors are separate and cannot become verified rationale exemplars. UNKNOWN is a permitted human failure-category judgment, not a synonym for a proven reasoning failure.
6. Import both sheets into a NEW bank revision. Two distinct attested humans approving the same source/candidate revision are required for VERIFIED. A negative judgment prevents verification. Do not resolve disagreement by discarding one sheet or editing a decision. A revised candidate needs a new revision and two new independent reviews; preserve the previous files.

The importer validates identities, attestations, exact source/candidate context, timestamps, hashes, spans, decisions and grounding booleans. It does not authenticate a person's real-world identity or automatically judge semantic entailment. Human attestations/reasons and review-file hashes remain auditable. Neither a matching quote nor a gold label establishes a grounded rationale automatically.

Unfilled templates are not completed reviews. Source records may be pending while suitable for provisional gold/wrong field inspection; the verified-rationale/mechanism interfaces stay blocked. The M1.6.2 common-bank ablations must later use exactly the same verified eligible IDs; this milestone does not run them.
